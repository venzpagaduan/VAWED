Our Little Reminders

Are children allowed at the wedding?

We truly cherish your little ones, but we have chosen to keep our wedding and reception an adults-only celebration, with the exception of immediate family. We kindly invite you to enjoy this occasion as a special date and a time to celebrate with us.

Can I bring plus one in your wedding?

We are keeping our wedding guest list very intimate, and invitations have been thoughtfully extended to each guest individually. In this case, just to you. We truly hope you can join us in celebrating this special day, as your presence would mean so much to us.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony will start promptly at 4:00 pm. We ask that you arrive 30 minutes early.

When is the RSVP deadline?

The RSVP deadline is on or before December 15, 2026.

What if I still attend even without submitting the RSVP?

As much as we would love to celebrate with everyone, if your RSVP was not submitted, unfortunately a seat and meal have not been reserved. We truly appreciate your thoughtfulness and hope you can join us by confirming in advance, so we may welcome you with the warmth and hospitality you deserve.

I replied “no” on the RSVP, but I changed my mind. Can I still attend?

When you RSVP “no,” your slot is given to another guest so seating and meals can be properly arranged. If you wish to attend after changing your response, please contact us directly to check if a slot is still available.

Do you have a gift preference?

As love is the heart of this day, your presence is truly the greatest gift we could ever receive. Should you wish to bless us further, we humbly suggest a monetary gift as we begin our new life together. Your kindness will help us build a home and future filled with love, joy, and lasting memories.

Angeline & Venz
